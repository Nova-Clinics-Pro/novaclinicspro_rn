## Organization evidence and reviewer authorization

### US-1.2 proposed amendment

> Public organization/clinic-owner registration shall collect business context
> required for component recommendation. Automatic approval requires applicable
> organization/clinic registration evidence validated through an owner-approved
> authority. Absent, unavailable, unsupported, ambiguous, or jurisdictionally
> unresolved evidence routes to manual review. Practitioner credential validation
> is deferred to clinical-role or privilege activation. Tax identifiers are
> optional unless governing jurisdictional policy explicitly requires them.

Minimum manual-review evidence is legal organization/clinic name, jurisdiction,
registration authority/type and identifier where applicable, opaque evidence
reference metadata, and applicant attestation. Human interpretation remains
manual review. This is a `PROPOSED_REQUIREMENTS_AMENDMENT`, not an adopted
requirement.

### US-2.1 proposed amendment

> Registration shall support risk-based automatic approval through a versioned
> hybrid policy. Automatic approval requires authoritative designated-contact
> evidence, applicable organization evidence, and owner-approved deterministic
> rules. Ineligible applications enter manual review, not automatic rejection,
> with safe notification and continuation. Decisions retain auditable
> system-or-human provenance and immutable policy version. Numerical scoring is
> diagnostic until separately calibrated and approved. Unsupported or
> discriminatory signals shall not determine approval.

The current reviewer route is not proven platform-wide. The narrow secure option
is an explicit platform review audience with approve/reject only, required
reason/actor/timestamp/audit, and no override of failed PF-018 evidence absent
an approved manual-verification lifecycle.

## C1 implementation-ready contract — contingent on OD-1 and OD-6

**Boundary:** only server-owned public-operation PF-018 composition and primary
contact designation. C2–C6, migration/provenance, public DTO privacy,
continuation UX, numerical calibration, and reviewer-permission changes are
excluded.

**Likely backend changes:** public registration request DTO/schema; a frozen
public-operation identity context; the existing
`IAuthoritativeIdentityProvider` and Supabase adapter; existing
`ClinicContactVerificationService` operation-scope validation/composition;
public-registration factory/service; existing B2 operation repository/UoW
composition; and narrowly scoped tests. The implementation reuses
normalization, evidence lifecycle, audit, and UoW. It creates no new provider,
evidence store, OTP system, parallel registration engine, or independent
transaction owner.

**Frontend change:** add a selection over already supplied clinic email/mobile
values; submit `primary_contact_kind`; do not duplicate contact inputs or show
provider confirmation details.

**Failure/replay:** provider mismatch, unconfirmed/ambiguous evidence, scope
failure, and unavailable authority produce safe manual review. Exact replay
returns the existing operation/decision correlation and must not issue,
consume, or regenerate evidence.

**C1 Definition of Done:**

- only the reconciled provider subject can be queried through the server-owned B2 context;
- designated same-kind contact exact-match uses PF-018 normalization;
- no fake organization/human actor or client-provided verification authority;
- replay remains idempotent without extra evidence consumption;
- no independent strategy commit or held external call inside local transaction;
- deterministic tests cover exact, mismatch, unconfirmed, unavailable, replay,
  subject mismatch, and authorization boundaries;
- owner-run isolated PostgreSQL proof covers operation/evidence correlation and
  rollback if persistence changes are authorized.
