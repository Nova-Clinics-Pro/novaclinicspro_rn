## 1. Owner-ratified hybrid direction

NovaClinics Pro shall retain genuine automatic approval through a hybrid policy:

1. authoritative exact-match verification of one explicitly designated primary clinic contact;
2. applicable organization-registration validation for automatic approval;
3. deterministic, explicitly approved MVP manual-review routing rules;
4. preservation of the existing scoring engine; and
5. future numerical approval authority only after evidence-backed calibration and separate owner approval.

Missing, unavailable, ambiguous, unconfirmed, or mismatched mandatory evidence, or an approved review trigger, routes an application to manual review with a safe actionable outcome. It does not cause automatic rejection merely because the applicant is ineligible for automatic approval.

This ratification does **not** approve the current `< 0.95` threshold, current weights, an exact factor list, a replacement rules platform, a new identity provider, OTP workflow, evidence store, migration, implementation, or test acceptance.

## 3. Requirements reconciliation

### US-2.1 — `PROPOSED_REQUIREMENTS_AMENDMENT`

Existing US-2.1 requires risk-based automatic approval, manual review, notification, and audit.

> Registration shall support risk-based automatic approval through a versioned hybrid policy. Automatic approval requires authoritative exact-match verification of the designated clinic contact, applicable authoritative organization-registration evidence, and owner-approved deterministic MVP eligibility/review rules. Ineligible applications enter manual review, not automatic rejection, with safe notification and continuation guidance. Decisions retain auditable system-or-human provenance and an immutable policy version. Numerical scoring may inform diagnostics but shall not authorize approval until governed factor, calibration, fairness, threshold, rollout, and rollback controls are separately approved. Unsupported or discriminatory signals shall not determine approval.

This preserves the approved outcome and scoring engine. It requires formal requirements-governance approval before replacing existing wording.

### US-1.2 — `PROPOSED_REQUIREMENTS_AMENDMENT`

Clinic-owner identity, organization/clinic registration, practitioner credentials, and clinical privileges are distinct. A clinic owner is not presumed to be a doctor; practitioner credentials are validated at clinical-role/privilege activation.

> Public organization/clinic-owner registration shall collect business context required for component recommendation. Automatic approval requires applicable organization/clinic registration evidence validated through an owner-approved authority. Absent, unavailable, unsupported, ambiguous, or jurisdictionally unresolvable evidence routes to manual review. Practitioner credential validation is deferred to clinical-role or privilege activation. Tax identifiers are optional unless a governing jurisdictional policy explicitly requires them.

Proposed MVP evidence is legal organization/clinic name, jurisdiction, registration authority/type and identifier where applicable, opaque evidence-reference metadata, and applicant attestation. No live authoritative organization validator is proven; this evidence cannot yet enable automatic approval.
