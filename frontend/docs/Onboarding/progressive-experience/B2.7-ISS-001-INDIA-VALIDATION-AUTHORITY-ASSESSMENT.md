## Recommended MVP validation model

**Model C: constrained hybrid authority model, pending external confirmation.**

1. Use HFR only for facility identity/listing after NHA confirms a production
   validation interface, purpose, access onboarding, matching semantics,
   retention, availability, and permitted data use.
2. Validate legal operating status through an official state clinical-establishment
   authority only when applicability, clinic category, and supported verification
   access are confirmed.
3. Automatic approval may cover only the approved state/category subset. All
   other India applications receive manual review, never automatic rejection.
4. Do not silently restrict nationwide product availability to a single state.

No national single authority is proven to validate all required facts. HFR alone
is insufficient. Model B state adapters remain possible but require selected
state authorities and access confirmation.

## Minimum automatic-approval contract

AUTO_APPROVAL_ELIGIBLE requires all of:

- PF-018 exact verification of the designated contact;
- approved jurisdiction, establishment category, and authority coverage;
- official validation of applicable legal registration and organization-name
  matching;
- approved applicant-representative authority evidence;
- no approved deterministic manual-review trigger;
- frozen policy, source/method, checked-at/expiry, safe reason, opaque evidence,
  ordered factors, and null human reviewer;
- replay-safe stored decision with no duplicate work.

MANUAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED covers missing, ambiguous, mismatched, expired, or
inconclusive evidence and approved review rules. AUTHORITY_UNAVAILABLE covers
provider outage/access failure. UNSUPPORTED_VALIDATION_COVERAGE covers an
unapproved state/category/authority. None causes automatic rejection.

## Proposed minimal adapter

IOrganizationRegistrationValidator is a proposed domain/application port, not
implemented code. Request: B2 operation correlation, normalized legal name,
approved jurisdiction/category/authority, registration identifier where
applicable, and policy version. Result: verified, not_verified, unavailable,
unsupported, or ambiguous; source/method; opaque evidence reference; safe match
summary/reason; registration status; checked-at; and expiry/revalidation rule.

The application service owns decision policy. An infrastructure adapter owns
external access and cannot approve, create tenants, grant roles, or commit.
Existing UoW owns local decision/audit/outbox/B2 completion. External access
must occur outside held database locks. Deterministic tests mock all outcomes;
approved persistence change requires owner-run isolated PostgreSQL proof.

## Applicant experience

or approved continuation. Do not disclose score, factors, registry responses,
evidence references, or security rules.
